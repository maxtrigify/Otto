- Say that your project is "based on Otto" or "a fork of Otto".

- Build and run Otto from source for yourself or your organisation.

- Name a fork, product or service "Otto", or anything confusingly similar.

If you publish a fork, please give it a different name and icon, and change the bundle
identifiers (`io.openotto.*`, and the legacy `app.muse.*` they replaced) so it doesn't collide with official builds.
